How to play

  1. Change your starting word into POOP, changing exactly one letter per move.
  2. Use the letter tiles and keyboard; each step must be an accepted four-letter word.
  3. Tap an earlier word in your ladder to rewind. Rewinds do not affect your ordinary score.
  4. Each Daily Poop has at least one shortest ladder avoiding the words we have flagged as obscure.
  5. Complete any shortest ladder for a rainbow. Earn the golden poop by solving today's daily puzzle perfectly on your first completed attempt, without rewinding.
  6. Daily Poop changes at midnight Eastern Time. Random Poop is unlimited. Switching modes preserves your progress.

The shortest-path solver uses our curated 3,103-word dictionary. Daily and Random starting words come from the same approved pool. Unusual words may still be valid moves; flagged obscure words are avoided in at least one shortest solution for every approved starting word.
